== Air-gapped enviroments

An "air-gapped" system or network is physically and logically isolated from
unsecured networks, including the internet. By creating a literal "air gap"
between secure systems and potential threats, unauthorized remote access and
cyberattacks are prevented, with data transfers requiring physical media like
USB drives.

=== Key Characteristics & Uses

* **Physical Isolation:** The computer or network has no wired or wireless
  connections (such as Wi-Fi or Bluetooth) to the outside world.
* **High Security:** Used primarily for critical infrastructure (like power
  plants), national defense, and storing highly sensitive data.
* **Air-Gapped Backups:** In enterprise data protection, companies maintain
  air-gapped backups to prevent ransomware from wiping out recovery data during
  an attack.
