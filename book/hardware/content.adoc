== Hardware

This book uses inexpensive hardware on purpose.
The goal is not to study the board for its own sake, but to learn embedded concepts that apply far beyond a single device.

A small and accessible platform keeps the examples concrete.
It lets the reader see real constraints, real interfaces, and real trade-offs without needing expensive equipment or a large lab setup.
That makes it easier to focus on the ideas that matter: firmware structure, timing, peripherals, testing, and automation.

The same ideas scale.
Once they are understood in a simple environment, they can be carried into larger projects, more complex boards, and stricter production workflows.
Learning the pattern in the small gives the reader a way to recognize it in the large.
